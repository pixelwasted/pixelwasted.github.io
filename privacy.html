Pixel Wasted Poster - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Pixel Wasted Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pixel Wasted creator account (@pixel.wasted) to publish short gaming video clips to that same TikTok account.

2. Data we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information of that account (open ID, display name and avatar), and the permission to publish videos (video.publish).

3. How we use it

This data is used only to publish videos to the owner's own account and to check the status of each upload. The app does not access, collect or store data from any other TikTok user.

4. Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system keychain. No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

5. Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions > Apps and services). Revoking access deletes the token's validity immediately.

6. Contact

pixel.wasted on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
